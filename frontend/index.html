What FinPal does
Everything a student budget needs, in one place
Talk naturally — FinPal fills in a real financial picture behind the scenes and turns it into a dashboard you can act on.
🎤

Voice-first advisor

Hold the mic and talk like you would to a friend. FinPal listens, replies out loud, and keeps a full transcript you can revisit anytime.

📊

Live budget dashboard

Every answer updates a visual snapshot — spending by category, income allocation, fixed vs. variable costs, all in real time.

⚠️

BNPL & debt risk flags

FinPal watches for missed payments, stacked buy-now-pay-later apps and high-interest debt, and tells you what to fix first.

🔒

Emergency fund tracking

Sees how many months of essentials your savings actually cover, and sets a target based on how stable your income is.

📈

Savings projection

Turns your monthly surplus into a graduation-day corpus estimate, assuming a realistic 12% p.a. index-fund return.

🎯

Goals & savings tracker

Mention a goal in conversation — a laptop, a trip, an emergency cushion — and FinPal keeps it visible on your dashboard.
